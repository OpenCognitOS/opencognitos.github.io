Questions about usage

Where do the numbers come from?

From the local logs of Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and Kimi Code on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Is the API value what I pay?

No — it’s what the same usage would cost through the API. On a subscription you pay your plan; the difference is your savings.

Does it count Hermes?

Not yet — Hermes keeps no readable usage log.

Can I bill clients with it?

Yes. Filter by project and export a CSV with tokens and API value per day, CLI and model.

What does it cost?

Free with up to 4 agents and 2 projects. Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription on top of your plan.
